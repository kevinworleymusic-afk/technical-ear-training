## Current Interpretation

Four documented continuation sessions have established baselines in Chapters 2 and 5. EQ work on pink noise remains the strongest chapter-based area. Session 2 established direct Q = 2 matching results; the August 2 session established Q = 8 identification results; and August 5 extended the record to one-third-octave pink-noise choices and the first scored “Fast Car” music-transfer blocks. The next step is delayed retention under the new pink-noise condition and staged music transfer. Sine-wave hard-clipping matching remains close to its working checkoff criterion. EQ cuts, pink-noise clipping, and bit-depth reduction remain active development areas. Compression and reverberation require direct parameter exploration before formal quiz-style testing; Mid/Side, expansion, and editing have not yet been scored.

These results describe performance only for the specific sources, settings, and exercises logged. They do not imply mastery across all program material or listening systems.
