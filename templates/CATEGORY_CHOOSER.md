# Ear-Training Category Chooser

Choose from these broad categories for a daily warmup, focused development block, or weekly lesson plan. You only need to name the category; the detailed plan will be populated from your current progress, prior session evidence, and the corresponding chapter.

| Category to type | Chapter | Software | What the generated plan may train |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **EQ** | 2 | TETpracticeEQ | Frequency-band boosts and cuts, single- or multi-band changes, matching, recall, rapid identification, and transfer from pink noise to music |
| **Mid/Side** | 3 | TETlisteningMidSide | Center-versus-side content, width, image position, focus, and stability |
| **Reverberation** | 3 | TETpracticeReverb | Decay, early reflections, room size, distance, and spectral character |
| **Compression** | 4 | TETpracticeComp | Attack, release, transient shape, envelope, sustain, and dynamic-range change |
| **Expansion** | 4 | TETpracticeExp | Below-threshold attenuation, ambience reduction, envelope change, and transient contrast |
| **Distortion** | 5 | TETpracticeDist | Hard clipping, bit-depth reduction, severity, harmonics, and source-dependent artifacts |
| **Editing** | 6 | TETpracticeEditing | Clicks, discontinuities, timing errors, truncation, ambience changes, and unnatural transitions |

## Simple Input Format

> **Warmup:** [category] and [category]  
> **Development:** [category]

Example:

> **Warmup:** EQ and distortion  
> **Development:** compression

You may also specify only one warmup category, add an optional rotating category, or keep the same development category across multiple days.

## What Gets Populated

From those broad choices, the daily session log can be populated with:

- exact warmup minutes totaling 12, 13, or 14 minutes;
- relevant software and source material;
- exercise mode and task type;
- processing settings and controlled variables;
- listening cues and likely confusions;
- staged development instructions;
- advancement decisions and stopping rules;
- results fields appropriate to scored or exploratory work.

The weekly plan stores the broad category choices. The dated session log stores the detailed implementation.
