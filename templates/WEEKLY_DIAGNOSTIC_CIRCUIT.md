# Weekly Full-Chapter Diagnostic Circuit

Complete this circuit approximately once per week to gauge broad progress across the available technical-ear-training software. Unlike the adaptive daily warmup, this session touches every chapter and preserves enough time to distinguish an unfamiliar cue from a temporary bad trial.

The long-term objective is working competence across all chapter areas. Weekly results identify which skills are established, which need focused development, and which are ready to move from reference exposure into the daily warmup. Strong areas remain active for retention while focused sessions push weaker areas forward.

## Purpose

- Measure broad week-to-week development
- Revisit skills that are not yet part of the stable daily warmup
- Identify the next primary-focus chapter
- Track whether established cues remain reliable
- Keep matching, memorized identification, rapid identification, and exploratory listening separate
- Document transfer to new source material without assuming it

## Interpretation Rules

- Time blocks control session length; trial count is an observed output.
- Compare scores only when source, settings, task type, and timing are sufficiently similar.
- Do not compare exploratory listening directly with scored identification.
- Do not pool matching, memorized identification, and rapid-identification scores.
- A poor unfamiliar-category score is a diagnostic signal, not evidence of regression.
- Add music or speech as separately documented transfer conditions after controlled cues stabilize.
