# Technical Listening Library

This index organizes the concepts and reference material used by the exercises. Add only material that supports a defined listening goal, and note the source and intended use.

## Frequency and Timbre

Topics:

- Octave and one-third-octave band recognition
- Narrow-band boosts and cuts
- Tonal balance
- Resonance, masking, and spectral density
- Harmonic content and timbral change

## Dynamics and Level

Topics:

- Small level differences
- Peak level versus perceived loudness
- Compression ratio, attack, and release
- Limiting and transient modification
- Envelope and crest factor

## Distortion and Artifacts

Topics:

- Harmonic and intermodulation distortion
- Clipping
- Saturation
- Aliasing
- Noise, hum, buzz, clicks, and dropouts
- Codec and low-bitrate artifacts

## Space and Acoustics

Topics:

- Localization and image stability
- Width, depth, and envelopment
- Early reflections
- Reverberation time and character
- Comb filtering and phase-related coloration
- Listening-position effects

## Loudspeakers and Systems

Topics:

- On-axis versus off-axis response
- Crossover-region behavior
- Driver integration
- Subwoofer localization and integration
- Level matching
- Seat-to-seat consistency
- Translation between playback systems

## Automotive Audio

Topics:

- Left/right asymmetry from the driver position
- Staging height, width, and center-image stability
- Tonal balance across seats
- Delay and level effects
- Cabin resonances and boundary effects
- Road-noise masking
- Bass integration
- Evaluation of tuning changes
