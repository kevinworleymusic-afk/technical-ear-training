## Reference-Track Rotation

Use the seven selected recordings as an approximately one-track-per-day weekly rotation. The first four come from Harman’s 2011 [*How to Listen* program-material list](https://harmanhowtolisten.blogspot.com/2011/01/welcome-to-how-to-listen.html); the remaining three appear in Harman’s [Professional Reference Songs](https://artoflistening.harman.com/professional-reference-songs) collection. This rotation and its daily scheduling are Kevin’s own practice design. Track-to-day assignments may shift between weeks, while Sunday remains the recurring diagnostic day with a reference-track warmup followed by standardized testing.

## Monthly Focus

- **Primary development category:** Equalization identification and controlled-to-music transfer
- **Secondary development category:** Distortion recognition and broader chapter familiarity
- **Warmup categories to maintain:** Pink-noise EQ boosts; established distortion cues
- **Categories to introduce or rotate:** Music-based EQ, compression, reverberation, Mid/Side, expansion, and editing
- **30-minute diagnostic date(s):** Pending; August 5 was a focused EQ benchmark, not the full Chapters 2–6 diagnostic
- **45-minute diagnostic date(s):** Pending
- **End-of-month target:** Demonstrate delayed retention for fine-frequency pink-noise identification and expand music-transfer evidence without conflating the two source conditions
