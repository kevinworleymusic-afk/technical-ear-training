## Entry Format

- **Warmup:** Enter one or two broad categories and the number of minutes assigned to each. The generated daily timetable must still total exactly 12, 13, or 14 minutes, including audio check and summary.
- **Development:** Enter one broad category.
- **Target:** Add one short instructional blurb, such as “stabilize three-band matching” or “learn attack/release cues.”
- **Daily log:** Add the dated log link after its detailed session plan is generated. Leave days without a planned session visually blank except for the date.
