## Phase Purpose

From July 18 through August 3, the calendar records a continuous **Critical Listening System Development** period. July records the setup of the documented training system: establishing baseline exercises, separating matching from memorized or rapid identification, defining comparable trial conditions, creating cumulative progress tracking, and beginning the warmup, diagnostic, and lesson-planning processes.

## Documented July Outcomes

- Two continuation-phase sessions were documented.
- The continuation phase reached 733 scored trials.
- EQ, distortion, compression, and reverberation work were brought into the documented process.
- Multi-band +6 dB pink-noise EQ matching was established at 97% across 30 two-band trials and 90% across 10 three-band trials.
- The distinction between matching, memorized identification, rapid identification, and exploratory listening was formalized.
- The daily warmup, focused-development, weekly diagnostic, and calendar-planning structures were established.
