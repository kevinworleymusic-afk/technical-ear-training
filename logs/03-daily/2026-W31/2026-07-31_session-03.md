## Development Block — Compression and Reverberation Workflow

This block is untimed. Its purpose is to make the exercise procedure operational and repeatable, not to claim mastery.

### Required Outcome

Complete at least one of the following workflows. Attempt both if listening quality and attention remain reliable.

### Workflow A — Compression

1. Choose and document the source material.
2. Establish level-matched unprocessed and compressed references.
3. Select one primary variable to train first; hold the others constant.
4. Explore clearly separated settings and write the audible cues before scoring anything.
5. Define the comparison method, feedback method, and repeatable trial procedure.
6. Run a short pilot and record whether the exercise works as intended.
7. Save the usable settings and identify the next difficulty step.
