## Procedure for Each Unfamiliar Chapter

1. Open and identify the available exercise controls.
2. Choose one narrow listening variable.
3. Establish a clear reference and comparison method.
4. Try a small pilot without treating exploration as a formal score.
5. Record the audible cue, software issue, or conceptual blocker.
6. Retain, revise, or defer the candidate.
7. Assign an initial warmup duration only after the procedure is usable.
