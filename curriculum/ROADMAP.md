# Technical Ear Training Roadmap

## Stage 1 — Vocabulary and Large Contrasts

Build a reliable descriptive vocabulary using obvious, single-variable changes.

- Octave-band EQ identification
- Large level differences
- Obvious compression and clipping
- Basic left/center/right localization
- Short versus long reverberation

**Completion evidence:** consistent session logs and at least 80% accuracy on repeated controlled trials.

## Stage 2 — Smaller Changes and Confidence

Reduce the size of changes and track whether confidence matches accuracy.

- ±3 dB EQ changes
- Small loudness differences
- Attack and release comparisons
- Width and depth judgments
- Common noise and artifact identification

**Completion evidence:** accuracy results by category plus a summary of recurring errors.

## Stage 3 — Complex Program Material

Apply isolated skills to real music and speech.

- Multi-attribute listening
- Level-matched processing comparisons
- Tonal-balance evaluation
- Translation across playback systems
- Listening-position comparisons

**Completion evidence:** short case studies that distinguish observations from explanations.

## Stage 4 — Measurement Correlation

Test listening judgments with objective data.

- Frequency response
- Spectrum and spectrogram
- Level and crest factor
- Distortion measurements
- Impulse response and decay
- Interchannel level, phase, and correlation

**Completion evidence:** reports comparing initial listening predictions with measured results.

## Stage 5 — Automotive Application

Apply the framework in vehicle cabins.

- Driver-seat staging
- Seat-to-seat consistency
- Cabin resonance recognition
- Delay, level, and EQ comparisons
- Bass integration
- Road-noise masking
- Repeatable system-evaluation reports

**Completion evidence:** an automotive-audio listening and measurement case study suitable for the engineering portfolio.
